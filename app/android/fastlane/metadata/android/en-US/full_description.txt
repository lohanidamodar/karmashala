Karmashala runs coding-agent CLIs on your computer. This app puts them in your pocket.

Pair your phone with the Karmashala server on your computer, your WSL box or a machine you reach over SSH, and every session it runs is one tap away. Sessions that need you come first.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See every session at a glance: working, waiting on you, finished or failed.
• Answer approvals and questions: allow, deny, or deny and say why.
• Read the chat and send messages, with files from your phone or from the server, or a photo.
• Open a session's terminal, drawn at the session's own size, with pan, pinch-zoom and an extra-keys row for Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt and the arrows.
• Browse the session's changes, history and files.
• Start new sessions and add projects.
• Get notified while the app is open when a session needs you, finishes or fails.

WORKS WITH
Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity CLI sessions run through Karmashala on your computer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your phone talks to your own machine over an end-to-end sealed channel, directly on your network or through a relay. Your code and your sessions stay on your machine. What each paired phone may do is granted on the computer and can be changed or revoked at any time.

YOU NEED
Karmashala running on a computer you own. Pair by scanning its QR code, pasting a code, or typing its address.
